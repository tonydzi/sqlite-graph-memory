# Bramble team

The Bramble team is based in [[site-ketterby|Ketterby]]. The Bramble team is headed by [[person-saskia-brannock|Saskia Brannock]]. The team was formed in 2010.

The Bramble team maintains [[tool-cobbleset|Cobbleset]] and [[tool-fathomer|Fathomer]].
