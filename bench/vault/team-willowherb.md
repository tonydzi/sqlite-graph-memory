# Willowherb team

The Willowherb team is based in [[site-brackwater|Brackwater]]. The Willowherb team is headed by [[person-rurik-pellegrin|Rurik Pellegrin]]. The team was formed in 2006.

The Willowherb team maintains [[tool-ferrodb|Ferrodb]] and [[tool-lanternfish|Lanternfish]].
